EMPLOYEE PAYROLL POLICY

Salary Date:
Employee salaries are processed on the last working day of each month.

Payslips:
Employees can access their monthly payslips through the employee portal.

Bank Account:
Employees must provide valid bank account details to the HR or payroll team before their first salary is processed.

Salary Questions:
Employees who notice an issue with their salary or payslip should contact the payroll team.

Tax Information:
Employees must provide the required tax declarations and supporting documents within the timelines communicated by the payroll team.

Salary Changes:
Any approved salary changes will be reflected in the payroll cycle communicated by HR.
