WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Eligibility:
Employees who have completed their probation period may request work from home, subject to their role and manager approval.

Frequency:
Eligible employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week.

Approval:
Employees must obtain approval from their reporting manager before working from home.

Request:
Work-from-home requests must be submitted through the company's employee portal at least 1 working day in advance.

Exceptions:
Employees may request additional work-from-home days in exceptional circumstances. Such requests require approval from the department manager.

Availability:
Employees working from home must remain available during normal working hours and attend scheduled meetings.
