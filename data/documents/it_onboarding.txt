IT ONBOARDING POLICY

Laptop:
New employees will receive a company laptop on their first working day.

Company Email:
A company email account will be created by the IT team on or before the employee's joining date.

VPN Access:
Employees who need access to internal company systems must request VPN access through the IT service portal.

Password:
Employees must change their temporary password after their first login.

IT Support:
For technical issues, employees should create a ticket through the IT service portal.

Laptop Issues:
If a company laptop has a hardware problem, employees should contact the IT support team rather than attempting repairs themselves.
