EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Casual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 6 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Applying for Leave:
Employees must submit their leave request through the company's employee portal.

Approval:
Leave requests must be approved by the employee's reporting manager.

Notice Period:
For planned leave of 3 or more consecutive working days, employees should submit the request at least 5 working days in advance.
