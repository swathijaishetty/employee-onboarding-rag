EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Working Hours:
The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Attendance:
Employees are expected to record their attendance using the company's attendance system.

Employee ID:
Employees must carry or display their company identification when required on company premises.

Code of Conduct:
Employees are expected to behave professionally and respectfully toward colleagues, customers, and business partners.

Confidentiality:
Employees must protect confidential company information and must not share confidential information with unauthorized individuals.

Security:
Employees must follow company information security policies and report suspected security incidents to the IT security team.

Reporting Manager:
Employees should contact their reporting manager for questions related to their day-to-day work responsibilities.
