EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY

Health Insurance:
Eligible full-time employees are covered under the company's group health insurance plan.

Insurance Enrollment:
New employees must complete health insurance enrollment within 30 days of their joining date.

Dependents:
Employees may add eligible dependents to the health insurance plan according to the terms of the insurance provider.

Reimbursement:
Employees can submit eligible medical reimbursement claims through the employee benefits portal.

Wellness Benefits:
Employees are eligible for the company's wellness benefits program after completing their probation period.

Questions:
Employees with questions about benefits should contact the HR benefits team.
